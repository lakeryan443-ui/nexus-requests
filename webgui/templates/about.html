About Nexus Requests

Media request management, scheduling, and priority downloads.

What is Nexus Requests?

Nexus Requests provides a central interface for managing media requests, scheduling downloads, and controlling request approvals through Discord.

Remote Request Management

Nexus Requests integrates with Discord to deliver media request notifications directly to your server. Administrators can approve, decline, or prioritize requests using interactive buttons without opening the Seerr app. The Approve & Download Now option allows approved requests to begin downloading immediately, even outside scheduled download hours.

Download scheduling

Nexus Requests gives you complete control over when your media downloads are allowed to run. Unlike qBittorrent's built-in bandwidth scheduler, which switches between normal and alternative download speed limits, Nexus Requests can automatically pause and resume downloads based on your configured schedule. By controlling when downloads actually run instead of simply limiting their speeds, Nexus Requests offers more precise scheduling and greater flexibility than qBittorrent's native bandwidth scheduler.

Priority downloads

Approve & Download Now allows approved requests to bypass the regular download schedule.

Connected services

Seerr, Radarr, Sonarr, qBittorrent, and Discord work together to manage media requests and downloads.

Future versions of Nexus Requests are planned to support additional download clients.
